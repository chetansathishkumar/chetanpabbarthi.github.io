About

I started with zero technical background and worked backward from one goal — becoming job-ready as a data & business analyst. A year at CubeSimple in Chennai gave me the first real test of that: automating ServiceNow workflows, analyzing over 40,000 support tickets for recurring patterns, drafting statements of work, and presenting findings to people who needed answers, not spreadsheets.

Since then I've been building outward from that base — SQL, Power BI, Python and Excel projects modeled on real business problems: delivery operations, insurance claims, digital marketing spend, B2B lead pacing. Each one is on this page, not a course certificate but a working file.

I hold a B.Tech in AI & Data Science from Saveetha Engineering College, and I'm currently looking for Data Analyst / Business Analyst roles where I can keep doing this at a larger scale.
